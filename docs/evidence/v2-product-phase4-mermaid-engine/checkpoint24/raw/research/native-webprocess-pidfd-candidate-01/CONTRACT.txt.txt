Sender and failure boundary
C invokes pidfd_open(getpid(),0), never sends an inner getpid as an external identity. On success it appends to a new GUnixFDList, requires index0/length1, closes its original FD and sends exactly1FD via asynchronous WebKitUserMessage. It releases its local list reference after send; the transport must own its reference. No response wait/retry. On syscall failure it sends0FD plus nativeStage1 and exact errno; on append failure it sends0FD plus nativeStage2 and GError domain/code, without inventing errno. Exactly one diagnostic attempt includes failure cases. Tuple (ssiiuittt):instance,token,stage,errno,GErrorDomain,GErrorCode,callbackStartNs,callbackEndNs,pageId. Payload limit1024bytes. Start/end cover the pidfd preparation only, not asynchronous delivery. Sandbox pidfd_open and actual descriptor transport are UNVERIFIED. Missing message may be transport failure, syscall restriction preventing delivery, process termination or other gaps; do not invent a reason without evidence.

Overhead and incompleteness
All helper directory/stat/proc/serialization/write/link work is synchronous, including GTK receive callback publication. Bytes/counts are bounded, elapsed time is NOT bounded: filesystem I/O, scheduling and syscalls may block, despite no deliberate sleeps/waits. captureEndNs is before serialization/write/link; it is NOT end-of-publication/callback or a complete overhead measurement. No callback stderr/eprintln remains (removed before freeze). prepare failure still emits at most one concise errno line per gated helper; stderr can block, and future run preflight must establish driver stderr is the run-owned ordinary log file, not claim that regular files are nonblocking. Publication failure is intentionally swallowed to preserve product behavior; absent final result is an explicit observation gap, and its exact I/O errno may be unavailable. No zero-perturbation claim. Diagnostic errors never propagate into guard-ready/normal rendering. Gates do not extend original deadlines or assertion timing.
