Run06 CPU review: correction to wire final timestamp terminology

This addendum corrects two interpretations in the frozen run06-review/REVIEW.txt (SHA85a14552ea05e32223da59ac09a758605d83fd39145bf8ee0f3e294d61c818cd), whose original20-file review-manifest.json remains SHA324d8b34eca71d044f04a3e96f2b23a284f48c3aec2d1d2221d1fddcd834f8d8. Neither that report nor any frozen input/output has been edited. This is a semantic correction only; no arithmetic, test or runtime was rerun.

The timestamp95324334286607 in native-capture/complete.json is the App wire writer's final publication checkpoint/sample. It is NOT a measured instant of actual diagnostic closure, stage closure, request settlement, or application exit. The actual close operation has no separately retained exact monotonic timestamp. A final/diagnosticClosed flag does not give this publication sample the semantics of that earlier operation; this addendum does not claim an exact filesystem publication completion time either.

Correction1 — original REVIEW.txt line39 (initial/final gaps)
Replace the interpretation beginning "Saved wire capture closes at App-local95324334286607" with: "The saved App wire writer final publication checkpoint is95324334286607. Its numerical difference from the last valid WebProcess sample read-end is181.920949ms. This is a sample-to-final-checkpoint comparison, conditional on unverified App/observer clock-coordinate equality; it does not measure sample-to-actual-close time. The guardian's final sample is numerically later than the wire final checkpoint, not evidence of its timing relative to actual close. Neither checkpoint supplies a request endpoint or delimits observer lifetime."

Correction2 — original REVIEW.txt line45 (render_6)
Replace "it is capture closure, not a terminal result" with: "it is the App wire writer final publication checkpoint, not a measured actual-close instant or a terminal result." The already reported200.909394ms is the same-App dispatch-to-final-checkpoint difference only. The frozen quantitative-summary.json field lastSampleToWireCloseNumericNs (and the matching wording in summarize_saved_report.py/gapsQualification) inherited the same imprecise label. Every value in that field is only final-checkpoint monotonicNs minus the corresponding sample read-end, under the same unverified cross-process coordinate assumption. It is not an actual-close gap. The frozen bytes and computed values are preserved; consumers must apply this correction rather than treat the field name as evidence of an independently timestamped close operation.
